# CERELO V1 — Pre-Deployment Legal & Regulatory Checklist

> **Document Status**: PRE-DEPLOYMENT RELEASE GATE  
> **Rule**: All items must be signed off by executive management and Nigerian legal counsel before commercial launch. Open items remain unchecked (`[ ]`).

---

## 1. Corporate Identity & Operating Entity

- [ ] **1.1 Corporate Entity Verified**: Registered company name verified with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC).
- [ ] **1.2 Registered Business Address**: Physical office address in Nigeria confirmed for legal service.
- [ ] **1.3 Corporate Identifiers Documented**: CAC Registration Number (RC) and Tax Identification Number (TIN) recorded.

---

## 2. Sectoral Courier & Logistics Licensing (NIPOST / CLRD)

- [ ] **2.1 Licence Category Confirmed**: Regional Courier / Logistics Licence category confirmed with the Courier and Logistics Regulatory Department (CLRD) of NIPOST for the Kano ↔ Katsina corridor.
- [ ] **2.2 Licensing Action Completed**: Direct operating permit or accredited licensed partner agreement finalized.
- [ ] **2.3 Goods-in-Transit (GIT) Insurance Executed**: Mandatory cargo insurance policy active and claim terms understood.
- [ ] **2.4 Tariff & Prohibited Goods Schedules Filed**: Rate structure and prohibited items documentation prepared for regulatory filing.

---

## 3. Data Protection Governance (NDPA 2023 & NDPC GAID 2025)

- [ ] **3.1 NDPC / DCMI Classification Completed**: Multi-factor classification assessment (UHL/EHL/OHL) completed.
- [ ] **3.2 NDPC Registration Assessed / Completed**: Formal controller registration filed if required by classification.
- [ ] **3.3 DPO Obligation Determined**: Internal Data Protection Officer or external DPCO designated if required.
- [ ] **3.4 Annual CAR Filing Obligation Determined**: Compliance Audit Return filing schedule confirmed.
- [ ] **3.5 Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) Conducted**: Internal DPIA evaluating corridor delivery workflows completed.
- [ ] **3.6 Cross-Border Processing Safeguards Reviewed**: Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) evaluated with Supabase, Resend, Google, and Vercel.
- [ ] **3.7 Data Retention Schedule Approved**: Formal corporate retention policy adopted.

---

## 4. Communication Channels & Incident Response

- [ ] **4.1 Operational Privacy Mailbox**: Dedicated privacy channel (`privacy@cerelonet.com`) active and monitored.
- [ ] **4.2 Customer Complaints / Support Desk**: Dedicated operational support channel (`support@cerelonet.com`) active.
- [ ] **4.3 Data Breach Response SOP**: 72-hour regulatory notification workflow established.

---

## 5. Operational & Consumer Policies (FCCPA 2018)

- [ ] **5.1 Comprehensive Prohibited Items Policy Approved**: Formal restricted items schedule adopted by operations leadership.
- [ ] **5.2 Cargo Loss, Damage & Liability Policy Approved**: Compensation limits, valuation formulas, and claim reporting windows finalized.
- [ ] **5.3 Failed Delivery & Return Protocol Approved**: Standard operational procedure for undelivered parcels and re-attempts approved.
- [ ] **5.4 Post-Custody Cancellation Protocol Approved**: Fee and handling rules for parcels cancelled after pickup confirmation confirmed.

---

## 6. Formal Legal Approvals & Public Deployment

- [ ] **6.1 Privacy Policy Formally Approved**: Public draft at `/privacy` approved by Nigerian legal and privacy counsel.
- [ ] **6.2 Terms of Service Formally Approved**: Public draft at `/terms` approved by Nigerian legal counsel.
- [ ] **6.3 Effective Dates Configured**: Concrete publication dates entered in `lib/config/legal.ts` and draft banners toggled off.
- [ ] **6.4 Mobile App Legal Links Configured**: Mobile onboarding and settings screens linked to approved web policies.
