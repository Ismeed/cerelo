### Statutory Basis:
- Under Sections 814 and 815 of the Companies and Allied Matters Act (CAMA) 2020, where an incorporated company carries on business under a business name that does not consist of its true corporate name without any addition, registration of that business name with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) is a **statutory legal requirement**.
- Operating and contracting commercially under the public brand **CERELO** requires registering **CERELO** as a Business Name under Part E of CAMA 2020, with Swifter Ventures LTD as the registered corporate proprietor.
- **Trademark Registration**: Registering the CERELO wordmark and logo under Class 39 (Transport, packaging, and storage of goods; parcel delivery) with the Trademarks Registry of the Federal Ministry of Industry, Trade and Investment is a distinct, recommended intellectual property protection measure.

## 3. Nigerian Courier & Logistics Regulatory Framework (NIPOST / CLRD)

- **Statutory Authority**: Courier and Logistics Regulatory Department (CLRD) of NIPOST under the Nigerian Postal Service Act (Cap N127 LFN 2004).
- **Applicable Category**: **Regional Logistics Licence** (North-West Geopolitical Zone: Kano & Katsina).
- **Regulatory Clarification**: CERELO does **not** possess an exemption from CLRD licensing merely by utilizing third-party middle-mile conveyance or partnering with existing drivers/vehicles. Operating parcel collection, consolidation, tracking, and doorstep distribution constitutes courier/logistics operations under Nigerian law.
- **Official Statutory Fees**:
  - Non-refundable Application Fee: **₦20,000.00** (Payable via Remita TSA).
  - Regional New Licence Fee: **₦2,000,000.00** (Payable upon approval).
  - Regional Annual Renewal Fee: **₦440,000.00** (Payable annually).
  - Minimum Share Capital: Authoritative confirmation required from CLRD regulations.
  - Licence Validity: One (1) Calendar Year.
- **May 2026 Digital Policy & SLA**: Fully-online application via `operator.clrd.nipost.gov.ng` with an official **30-day complete-application decision SLA**.
- **Current Position**: **UNLICENSED / NOT YET APPLIED**. No application has been filed and no fees have been paid.

## 4. Goods-in-Transit (GIT) Insurance Status

- **Current Position**: **UNINSURED**. No commercial Goods-in-Transit (GIT) policy is currently bound with an underwriter.
- **Prohibition on Claims of Insurance**: Cerelo must **not** state, advertise, or imply to customers or the public that shipments or parcels are insured until an active policy with a NAICOM-registered insurer is executed and bound.
- **Internal Compensation Reserve Proposal**: The proposed ₦25,000 compensation reserve is an **unapproved internal risk management proposal only**, not insurance.
