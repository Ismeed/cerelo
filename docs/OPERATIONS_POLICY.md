## 1. Prohibited, Restricted & Conditionally Acceptable Items (PROPOSED)

### A. Prohibited Items (Zero Tolerance — Strictly Rejected at Inspection)
1. **Illegal Narcotics & Controlled Substances**: Cannabis, cocaine, heroin, tramadol, codeine syrups, and unprescribed psychoactive substances.
2. **Weapons, Firearms & Ammunition**: Military or civilian firearms, ammunition, airguns, hunting rifles, bows, arrows, and combat knives.
3. **Explosives & Pyrotechnics**: Fireworks, dynamite, detonators, black powder, flares, and ammunition components.
4. **Flammables & Combustible Liquids**: Petrol, diesel, kerosene, lighter fluid, paint thinners, and propane gas canisters.
5. **Toxic, Corrosive & Hazardous Chemicals**: Industrial acids, pesticides, poisons, radioactive substances, and biohazardous waste.
6. **Physical Cash, Currency & Monetary Instruments**: Unsealed cash notes, currency bricks, bearer bonds, traveler's cheques, and bullion.
7. **Live Animals & Plants**: Livestock, poultry, domestic pets, wild game, fresh animal carcasses, and invasive agricultural seedlings.
8. **Stolen & Counterfeit Goods**: Property identified as stolen, pirated media, and forged official documents/currency.

### B. Restricted Items (Acceptable Only with Explicit Prior Approval & Documentation)
1. **Prescription Pharmaceuticals**: Valid manufacturer packaging, pharmacy invoice, and patient/physician documentation.
2. **Sealed Commercial Packaged Foods**: Dry, non-perishable grains, packaged confectioneries, and sealed spices.
3. **High-Value Electronics**: Laptops, smartphones, and tablets with recorded serial numbers within standard size tiers.

### C. Conditionally Acceptable Items
1. **Textiles & Kantin Kwari Fabrics**: Wrapped securely in weather-resistant plastic wrapping.
2. **Footwear & Leather Goods**: Packaged in rigid shoeboxes or durable cartons.
3. **Hardware, Auto Spares & Tools**: Cleaned of all oil/grease, secured with protective end-caps.

## 2. Fragile & High-Value Items Policy (PROPOSED)

1. **Declared Fragility**: Senders must declare fragile contents during physical pickup inspection.
2. **Packaging Responsibility**: Senders are responsible for adequate internal protective padding.
3. **Proposed Acceptance Condition**: CERELO reserves the right to decline unpadded fragile items or accept them strictly at sender's risk until a formal insurance framework is established.

---

## 3. Sender Packaging Responsibilities (PROPOSED)

1. **Outer Packaging**: Senders are responsible for providing adequate outer packaging (box, carton, or heavy-duty sealed polythene).
2. **Personnel Verification**: Cerelo Personnel inspects package integrity at pickup before confirming custody.

---

## 4. Loss, Damage & Commercial Claims Framework (PROPOSED — UNAPPROVED)

1. **Proposed Claims Window**: Written notice within **7 calendar days** of delivery completion or notice of delivery exception.
2. **Proposed Liability Formula**: In the absence of an active commercial Goods-in-Transit insurance policy, CERELO proposed maximum liability is capped at **₦25,000 per parcel** or **5× the paid delivery charge**, whichever is lower.
3. **Status**: **UNAPPROVED MANAGEMENT PROPOSAL**. Requires formal legal and executive sign-off.

---

## 5. Failed Delivery, Receiver Unavailable & Return-to-Sender (PROPOSED)

1. **Proposed Attempt Protocol**: Up to **2 doorstep delivery attempts** per shipment.
2. **Proposed Receiver Call Protocol**: Up to **3 phone verification call attempts** spaced across 30 minutes before recording an attempt exception.
3. **Proposed Return-to-Sender (RTS) Rule**: Senders must pay the reverse transit delivery fee upon receiving the returned parcel at origin.

---

## 6. Sender Cancellation After Custody Handover (PROPOSED)

1. **Before Custody (`Confirm Parcel`)**: Free cancellation by Sender.
2. **After Custody Handover**: Cancellation incurs a return processing fee; parcels already in transit must complete transit to destination hub before reverse return.

---

## 7. Payment Dispute & Cash Collection Rules (PROPOSED)

1. **Strict Cash-Only**: All fees collected in Nigerian Naira (NGN) cash physically by authorized Personnel.
2. **No Credit Handover**: Personnel is strictly prohibited from releasing a parcel if the designated paying party refuses physical cash payment.

---

## 8. Abandoned Parcels Policy (PROPOSED)

1. **Holding Period**: Unclaimed or refused parcels held securely at operating hubs for **30 calendar days** following 3 documented formal contact attempts.
2. **Disposal**: Lawful disposal under supervisory audit in accordance with applicable Nigerian commercial law.

---
